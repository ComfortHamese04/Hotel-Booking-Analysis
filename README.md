# Hotel Booking Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses hotel booking data to identify the factors influencing occupancy, cancellations, customer behaviour, and revenue performance. The dataset was cleaned, transformed, analysed, and visualised in Power BI to convert booking records into practical business insights.

The analysis covers City and Resort hotels and gives decision-makers an interactive view of:

- Seasonal demand
- Guest segments
- Booking channels
- Room performance
- Meal preferences
- Cancellation patterns

## Business Problem

Hotels must balance occupancy and pricing while limiting cancellations and creating an experience that encourages guests to return.

Without a consolidated analytical view, management may struggle to answer important questions such as:

- When do bookings and revenue reach their highest levels?
- Which hotel and customer segments have the highest cancellation risk?
- Which booking channels and room types generate the most revenue?
- How do lead time, deposit type, and special requests affect cancellations?
- What actions can improve occupancy, customer retention, and profitability?

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate booking and revenue performance across the two hotel types.
- Identify seasonal booking patterns and peak demand periods.
- Investigate the leading causes and characteristics of cancellations.
- Analyse customer types, booking behaviour, meal preferences, and countries of origin.
- Determine the most valuable market segments and room types.
- Recommend practical actions to improve occupancy, retention, and revenue.

## Key Findings

### Booking and Seasonal Trends

- Booking activity peaks in **July and August**, particularly during weeks 31 and 33.
- Guests generally book further in advance during busy periods.
- Higher average daily rates during peak months appear alongside increased cancellations.
- This may indicate that some guests are sensitive to higher prices close to check-in.
- Revenue increased year over year and reached its highest levels during July and August.

### Cancellation Behaviour

- The City Hotel recorded the highest number of cancellations.
- This result is consistent with the City Hotel having a larger overall booking volume.
- New guests accounted for **98.91% of cancellations**.
- Bookings containing special requests were less likely to be cancelled.
- This suggests that guests making special requests may have a stronger commitment to their bookings.
- Longer lead times showed a slight relationship with increased cancellations.
- Guests booking far in advance may be more likely to cancel because their plans can change before arrival.

### Customer Behaviour

- **82.37%** of bookings came from transient customers.
- Transient-party guests recorded the highest average lead time.
- Contract customers had the longest average stays.
- **96.09%** of guests were new customers.
- The large number of new guests demonstrates strong customer acquisition but limited customer retention.
- Bed and Breakfast was the most popular meal plan.
- **98.69%** of bookings used the No Deposit option.
- This shows that most customers prefer flexible reservation options.
- Portugal was the leading guest country, followed by Great Britain, France, Spain, and the Netherlands.

### Revenue Performance

- Online Travel Agents generated **64.62% of total revenue**.
- Online Travel Agents were the leading market segment.
- Offline Travel Agents and Tour Operators were also important revenue contributors.
- Room Type A generated the largest share of room revenue.
- Revenue reached its highest levels during the peak booking months of July and August.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Use Seasonal Promotions

- Launch early-booking campaigns for July and August because customers tend to book further in advance during these months.
- Offer longer-stay discounts during quieter periods to improve occupancy outside peak weeks.

### 2. Reduce Preventable Cancellations

- Send automated booking reminders as the guest’s check-in date approaches.
- Introduce flexible pricing based on lead time and cancellation risk.

### 3. Provide Commitment-Based Booking Options

- Offer discounted, non-refundable rates where appropriate.
- Continue providing flexible booking options for customers who need them.
- Use discounted rates to encourage guests to commit to their reservations.

### 4. Increase Guest Engagement

- Create packages that include popular special requests and value-added services.
- Use these packages to improve customer commitment and increase revenue.

### 5. Strengthen Customer Retention

Introduce:

- A customer loyalty programme
- Discounts for returning guests
- Personalised post-stay emails
- Targeted promotional offers
- Rewards for direct bookings

These initiatives could encourage new guests to return.

### 6. Optimise Profitable Booking Channels

- Continue investing in Online Travel Agents because they generate the largest share of revenue.
- Encourage direct bookings to reduce overdependence on third-party booking platforms.

### 7. Increase Revenue per Booking

Create opportunities to upsell:

- Room upgrades
- Longer stays
- Meal packages
- Transport services
- Exclusive hotel experiences

### 8. Promote Preferred Packages

- Keep the Bed and Breakfast option clearly visible across all booking platforms.
- Bundle Bed and Breakfast with other services to create attractive packages for guests.
